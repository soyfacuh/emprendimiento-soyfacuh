— FAQ

Frequently asked questions about websites, marketing and training.

Timelines, technical details, SEO, campaigns, support and working remotely. If your question is not here, message me directly.

— Pricing, timelines and process

Pricing, timelines and process

How long does a website take?

Most focused projects are delivered in roughly 7–14 days once the scope and content are ready. Larger sites or integrations can take longer, and the timeline is defined before work starts.

How do payments work?

The payment structure depends on the project. You receive a clear proposal with scope, milestones and payment terms before development begins.

Do I get revisions?

Yes. Design and content decisions are reviewed during the project so we can correct direction before launch.

Is the discovery call free?

Yes. The initial call is free and without commitment.

— Websites, stores and technical questions

Websites, stores and technical questions

Can you build an online store?

Yes. I can build catalogs, stores and e-commerce experiences depending on how your business needs to handle products, payments and orders.

Can you fix a website built by someone else?

Yes. I can review bugs, speed, technical SEO, responsive issues, backups and migrations even when I did not build the original site.

Do you include hosting and domain?

Hosting can be included depending on the plan. Domains are usually purchased separately, but I can handle the technical connection and setup.

Will the website work on mobile?

Yes. Mobile usability is a core requirement, not an extra.

— SEO, campaigns and social media

SEO, campaigns and social media

Can you manage Meta Ads and Google Ads?

Yes. Campaign work can include structure, tracking, copy, creative direction, optimization and reporting.

Can you help me appear on Google?

Yes. SEO work can include technical SEO, local search, content structure, Google Business and Search Console. No one can honestly guarantee a specific ranking position.

Do you produce photos and videos?

My marketing service focuses on strategy, content planning, design and campaigns. If professional photo/video production is needed, it can be coordinated separately.

— Training and support

Support & language availability

Do you offer training in English?

Not currently. One-to-one classes and live training are offered in Spanish. Web development, websites and digital projects can be delivered in English or bilingually.

Can you build an English-language website?

Yes. I can build English or bilingual websites and work remotely with clients in the United States and other international markets.

Can you provide ongoing support?

Yes. Support can be hourly or ongoing depending on the website and the level of maintenance you need.

— Working together

Working together

Who will I speak with?

Directly with me, Facundo. There is no sales handoff or account manager between us.

Can you work with international clients?

Yes. I am based in Buenos Aires and work remotely with clients abroad, including US-based projects. Spanish is my first working language, while websites and digital deliverables can be produced in English.

How do we communicate?

WhatsApp works well for day-to-day updates, and we can use video calls when a larger decision or walkthrough is easier live.
