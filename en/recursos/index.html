What does a website need to rank in Google and AI-powered search?

The foundation is still a crawlable, fast, useful website with original content that answers a real need. There is no magic file that guarantees inclusion in AI answers.

What actually matters

  • Important pages must be crawlable and indexable.
  • Titles, headings and copy should clearly explain what you offer, for whom and where you work.
  • Original content: experience, processes, comparisons and useful answers.
  • Internal links connecting services, work, FAQ and resources.
  • Strong mobile performance and accessible navigation.
  • Optimized images with dimensions and descriptive alt text where useful.
  • Structured data that matches visible content.
  • Search Console and Analytics to measure impressions, queries, clicks and conversions.

What about AEO or GEO?

They can be useful ways to think about content: answer directly, make entities clear and add context a person or system can understand. They do not replace technical SEO or original content.

How much does a professional website cost in 2026?

There is no serious single price for every website. A landing page, catalog and e-commerce site solve different problems; the quote depends on scope, design, content, features and support.

What changes the quote

  • Type of site: landing page, company website, catalog, e-commerce or web application.
  • Design: adapting an existing direction is different from custom interface work.
  • Content: copy, photos, product uploads and information architecture.
  • Integrations: WhatsApp, forms, payments, bookings, Analytics, CRM or automation.
  • SEO: architecture, content, local visibility and measurement.
  • Maintenance: self-managed delivery or ongoing support.

The best starting point is the business objective, then the smallest solution that actually solves it. A focused website can outperform a larger project without a clear strategy.

Web strategy

Landing page, digital catalog or online store: which one should a business choose?

Choose based on the action you want the customer to take. If you need an inquiry, a landing page may be enough. If you need to show variety and close through messaging, a catalog can be simpler. If you need payments and order management, an online store makes more sense.

Landing page

Best for one service, campaign or focused offer with a clear conversion path.

Digital catalog

Best when there are several products or services but the sale still closes through WhatsApp or direct contact.

Online store

Useful when the business needs cart, payments, orders, stock or a more automated sales operation.

The better question

Do not start with “what kind of website is trendy?” Start with “what journey does my customer need to move from discovery to inquiry or purchase?”

How long does it take for a website to appear and rank in Google?

Indexing and ranking are different. Google may discover a page relatively quickly, but competitive rankings usually need time, relevant content, trust signals and improvements based on data.

Three different stages

Discovery

Google finds the URL through links, a sitemap or Search Console.

Indexing

The page may enter the index if it is accessible, useful and technically available.

Ranking

Position depends on the query, competition, relevance, quality and signals built over time.

Search Console is valuable here because it shows impressions, queries, average position and clicks. A query already close to page one can become a concrete optimization opportunity.

MarketingAdvertising

Meta Ads or Google Ads: which one makes more sense for a small business?

Google Ads often captures existing demand: someone is already searching for a solution. Meta Ads can create discovery and interest before an active search exists. The right choice depends on the business, intent and budget.

Google AdsCapture intent

Useful when people already search for the product or service and meaningful search demand exists.

Meta AdsCreate discovery

Useful for visual offers, specific audiences, remarketing and products that benefit from being seen before a search.

What should be measured?

Likes and clicks alone are not enough. Depending on the goal, useful metrics can include qualified inquiries, cost per lead, conversions and return.

What should a small-business website include to generate inquiries?

A strong business website needs to explain quickly what you do, who it is for, why someone should trust you and what the next action is. Visual design helps, but clarity and low friction often matter more.

  • A clear value proposition in the first screen.
  • Services explained in customer language.
  • Trust signals: work, experience, process or real references.
  • A visible call to action: WhatsApp, form, booking or purchase.
  • Local information when the service depends on location.
  • A carefully designed mobile experience.
  • Measurement of important actions so you know what converts.
ChecklistMobile first

Checklist before publishing a professional website

A website is not finished when it simply looks good. Before launch, make sure search engines can access it, the mobile experience works and there is a clear way to measure whether it generates inquiries.

  • Unique title and description per page.
  • One clear H1 and a logical H2/H3 hierarchy.
  • Consistent canonical, sitemap and robots.txt.
  • No broken internal or external links.
  • Optimized responsive images.
  • Buttons and forms usable on small screens and by keyboard.
  • Analytics and Search Console configured.
  • Structured data consistent with visible content.
  • A clear CTA: message, form, purchase, booking or the real business action.
